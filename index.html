A few reminders

None of these fix everything. They're just true.

01

You do not have to solve everything tonight.

Tonight only asks for the next small step — water, food, rest, a pause.

02

Your mind is not a reliable narrator.

When pain is loud it makes everything feel permanent. Feelings move.

03

Small good things are still waiting.

A song, a warm drink, a quiet room. Ordinary stuff still counts.

04

You have already survived what felt impossible.

Every hard season you got through is still yours.

05

Rest is not failure.

Stopping for a bit does not erase the work you've done.

06

You don't owe a performance of being okay.

It's fine if today is quiet and unfinished.

About

This is a small page for hard days. No accounts, no tracking, no advice that pretends to fix your life in five steps.

Just reminders, a breathing thing, and numbers you can call if you need a real person.

If you're here, I hope something on this page makes the next hour a little lighter.
